Frequently asked questions

Straight answers — optimized for quick reading and search.

What is BrainHero?

BrainHero is a playful iPhone app with quick brain training exercises for memory, logic, math, attention and reaction — built for short, satisfying sessions, daily challenges, streaks, XP and leaderboards.

How long is a session?

Most exercises are 1–3 minutes. Pick a category and difficulty and start instantly — ideal for breaks, commutes or quiet moments.

What can I play inside?

Four families: Card Match (find pairs, recall patterns), Logic (sort cargo, connect circuits, guide lasers), Math (arithmetic and number drills) and Attention & Reaction (respond to signals, spot targets).

What are Daily Challenges and streaks?

Each day brings a fresh Daily Challenge. Complete it to earn XP, extend your streak, level up on a colorful hero journey and climb the leaderboard.

Is BrainHero medical advice or does it make health promises?

No. BrainHero is designed for entertainment and skill-based practice. It does not provide medical advice and does not promise improvements to intelligence or health.

Is it free? Does it work offline?

BrainHero is free to download on iPhone (iOS 17+). Core play works offline; daily content and leaderboards update when you are online.
